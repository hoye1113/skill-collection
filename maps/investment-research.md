# 投研分析 Skill 地图

从财报读取到报告交付的完整投研工作流。

## 阶段 1：标的分析

- [business/financial-report-reader](../business/financial-report-reader/) — 深度解读标的财报三表
- [business/stock-finance-profiler](../business/stock-finance-profiler/) — 计算 20+ 财务指标 + 杜邦分析
- [business/value-invest-scorer](../business/value-invest-scorer/) — 巴菲特/格雷厄姆价值投资评分

## 阶段 2：深度研究

- [research/hv-analysis](../research/hv-analysis/) — 横纵分析法：纵向时间线 + 横向竞品对比
- [business/equity-researcher](../business/equity-researcher/) — 生成机构级深度研报

## 阶段 3：报告产出

- [writing/report-writing](../writing/report-writing/) — 端到端长报告排版与引用管理
- [productivity/pdf](../productivity/pdf/) — 导出 PDF 交付物
- [writing/copy-editing](../writing/copy-editing/) — 七轮精修报告文本

## 可选增强

- [research/market-research](../research/market-research/) — 验证行业 TAM/SAM/SOM
- [research/competitive-analysis](../research/competitive-analysis/) — 拆解同业竞争格局
- [business/pricing-strategy](../business/pricing-strategy/) — 分析标的产品定价策略
